## What's new in 0.5.57

- **New dances in blug.dj/jej:** the robot, the worm, breakdance, the moonwalk and the dab (side to side), as buttons in the room's actions and as `/robot`, `/worm`, `/breakdance`, `/moonwalk`, `/dab` in chat. **💤 Sleep** now lies you down on the floor.
- **Interface — threads stay open:** opening a thread while the list was refreshing could throw you back to the list a few seconds later (often just as you opened a picture). A refresh that lands late no longer draws over the thread you've opened.
- **Copy on right-click:** select text in the Interface (or any of the app's windows) and right-click it for **Copy**, instead of the window's menu.
