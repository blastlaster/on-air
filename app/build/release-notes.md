## What's new in 0.5.9

- **Auras.** In blug.dj/jej, open 🎨 Look and pick one: Glow, Super, Charge, Halo rings, Rainbow, Shadow or Ultra. Some come in colours, rainbow included. Wear one together with a glowstick, which has moved to Look too.
- **An actions panel.** Poses, jump, dash, fire, confetti and the rest now sit beside the room on a wide screen. Press ⌨ to choose your own keys for any of them.
- **Cooldowns you can see.** 🔥 and 🎊 grey out with a countdown until you can use them again.
- **Memeoji.** The station's own picture emotes are at the top of the 😀 Emote tab.
- **Le Radioman talks from his character.** Live, his chat comes from him at the desk; off air, he pops up in a corner of the room to talk.
- **Look closes when you get going.** Click the room or start walking and it closes (you can switch this off in Look).
- **Chats open at the newest message**, and stay there unless you scroll up to read.
