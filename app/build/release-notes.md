## What's new in 0.5.2

- **More to do in blug.dj/jej.** Challenge anyone to rock paper scissors (click them) — the whole room sees who threw what and who won. Five new moves (clap, point, spin, headbang, sleep), new outfits (hoodies, suits, dresses, beards, halos, horns, party hats and more) and lots more skin colours.
- **Carry things around.** The new 😀 button adds emoji to what you say, or puts one in your character's hand for everyone to see.
- **The chat stays.** blug.dj/jej keeps the whole conversation; move it (⇄) or resize it (– +), and hide the game results if you like.
- **FunpostTV on the stage** now has the station's sign scrolling underneath.
- **One chat.** Whenever the station's PC is on, the Chat window is the station's own chat, so you're talking with everyone in blug.dj/jej too.
