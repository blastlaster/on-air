## What's new in 0.5.47

- **Interface — draw in green threads:** 🎨 **Draw** opens an oekaki, like the board's own drawing tool: pencil, pen (follows tablet pressure), airbrush, screentone, eraser, bucket and pipette, a palette, layers, undo/redo and zoom. Put a drawing on a new thread or on any reply (a reply can be just a drawing). Drawings are the only pictures green threads take: nothing can be opened, pasted or dropped in.
- **Interface — green threads read like a text board:** a grey page, a red title, numbered posts, "Name: Anonymous : date".
- **[s4s] window — green threads on the board:** they're pinned on top of the index and catalog, with their drawings and last few replies. Click one to read it all and reply in the Interface. **[–]** hides one, **Hide all** hides them all.
