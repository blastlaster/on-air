## What's new in 0.5.39

- **Chat commands:** type /help in chat for the list.
  - /nick changes your name.
  - /afk or /brb puts an AFK flag over you in blug.dj/jej, and anyone who mentions you hears you're away. Ten minutes idle in the room does it too.
  - /me for actions, /np for what's playing, /request a song, /who's here.
  - /ignore someone to hide their messages (/unignore to undo).
  - /wave, /dance and the other emotes work from chat in blug.dj/jej.
- **/roll** a number. Dubs, trips, quads, straights, palindromes and more get cheered.
- **/fortune** rolls your fortune, in its colour.
