## What's new in 0.5.37

- **Held emotes move:** the station's GIF emotes play while someone holds them, and in chat bubbles too.
- **Hot potato, bigger boom:** whoever holds the bomb when it goes off flies across the room, lands charred, coughs while stunned, then shakes off the dust (no more halo).
- **Not straight back:** you can't pass the bomb right back to whoever just passed it to you; J passes it to someone else.
