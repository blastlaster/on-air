## What's new in 0.5.33

- **One window.** Settings → Layout → One window puts the whole station in a single window laid out like the website: Now Playing, the board or blug.dj/jej in the middle, the TV and chat on the side.
- **Calling in works from more places.** Calls now go through the station's relay when a direct line can't be made (for example on some home and mobile networks).
- **blug.dj/jej:** the bottle of water now shows on Windows 10 too.
