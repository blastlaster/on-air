## What's new in 0.5.62

- **🎮 Games:** click someone and one Games button opens every game. Star your favourites to keep them at the top, in your theme's colour. Air hockey asks which style when you challenge.
- **🏁 Super Nice Kart:** the kart racer is now races anyone can open. Public races: join while there's a seat (up to 8) or watch, and join mid-race to race the next one. Points for your place add up on a leaderboard race after race, everyone votes on the next track, and it keeps going until the last racer leaves. Practice on your own: one track as many times as you like, or a circuit of all six.
- **Nicer karts and tracks:** karts get a rear wing, racing stripes, a number plate, chrome exhausts and treaded tyres. Every track has its own scenery (speakers, palms, snowmen, giant burgers, silos, blinking towers…), chevrons on the tight bends, a START arch, painted grid boxes and asphalt.
