## What's new in 0.4.4

**📻 The radio**
- **⏭ Vote to skip.** When more than half of the people listening press ⏭, the AutoDJ song is skipped. A bar under the volume shows how close a vote is. (When the station allows it, and its PC is on.)
- **Long song titles scroll** instead of being cut off.
- **TV no longer keeps buffering** on Windows laptops with several windows open.
- **Updates bring you back.** After an update, On Air opens again by itself. If it updated as you quit, it asks first: *Open On Air* or *Close*.
- Tidier spacing at the top of the Playlist window.

**🟩 The Interface**
- **Search** the threads from the top bar.
- **Back to where you were:** leaving a thread returns you to the same spot in the list, and each thread remembers where you stopped reading.
- **🟩 Green only** inside a thread shows just the green posts.
- **Pictures:** add one to a green post (pick, paste or drop it). Save any picture with **↓ save**, or right-click it to save it, copy its address or search for it.
- **Options list:** click the Options field to see every option.
- **Checking on 4chan itself is worth double XP:** reply from the board window quoting someone's repeats.

**📋 The board**
- The board window now presents itself as plain Chrome. If 4chan still says your IP range is blocked, use its *verify your e-mail* link inside the board window.
- Four more suggested scripts: reply helper, Get Watcher, New [s4s] fortunes and [s4s] colored text.
