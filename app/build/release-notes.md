## What's new in 0.5.72

- **FunpostTV keeps playing through events:** when the video steps aside in blug.dj/jej (Burgertime, the go-live intro, the full-screen games), only the picture hides. The video, its sound and the request queue carry on, and the picture comes back after.
- **Video requests tidy up:** after a request goes through, or when you empty the search box, the old search results clear, both in blug.dj/jej's 📥 panel and in the Request window's 📺 Video tab. What you're typing no longer resets when the queue changes.
