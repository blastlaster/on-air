## What's new in 0.5.70

- **blug.dj/jej in 2.5D:** the same room, drawn as a real space. The stage, speakers and radio are solid things; the station floats in outer space among stars, a nebula and galaxies; club lights sweep the dance floor and flash on the beat, a mirror ball scatters light, and sound waves roll out of the speakers. Going live brings smoke on the stage, the desk landing in a flash and Radioman walking out under a follow spot. Everyone's in the same room whichever way they see it: you walk, talk and play together. The camera is the station's: it shows the whole room and moves only for a few seconds at a time (Dodgeball starting, or the host pointing everyone at the stage), and you can walk the whole time.
- **Which view:** 🎨 Look → View. *Auto* follows Effects (Simple: 2D; Mid and Advanced: 2.5D), or pick 2D or 2.5D yourself. It needs WebGL (nearly every computer has it); without it the room stays 2D. On battery, 2.5D draws 30 frames a second to save power.
- **Watch FunpostTV:** when it's on the stage, click its screen to take a seat in the front row. In 2.5D the view drops to your seat: the TV almost fills it off air, and on air the TV's on the left and Radioman on the right. Drag to look side to side, use the wheel (or a pinch) to zoom. 🍿 popcorn and 💧 a sip of water: everyone sees you snacking. Walking, Esc or *Stand up* gets you up, and so does anything you'd want to see: Dodgeball, Burgertime, the Radioman fight, a race or the UFO.
- **Fixed:** FunpostTV's picture no longer stays floating over the Radioman fight, races and air hockey. Air hockey's sparks and confetti no longer pile up when the window's hidden mid-game.
