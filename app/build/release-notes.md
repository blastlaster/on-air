## What's new in 0.5.56

- **blug.dj/jej — kart races:** when the host starts one, say *I'm in* (up to 8 racers) and race laps from behind your kart on one of six tracks (Neon Loop, Sunset Speedway, Frostbite Hairpins, Burger Boulevard, Crop Circle Raceway, Static Peaks). **Shift** drifts round corners and fills the nitro gauge, **Space** fires a nitro, and a tap on the gas as a drift ends is an instant boost. **⌨ Keys** changes any key. Everyone else watches; first over the line gets the gold star.
- **blug.dj/jej — Radioman, to the death:** the host summons him, the room shatters, and everyone flies a little plane against a giant Le Radioman: radio waves, notes, poop grenades, static, spirals and a fart blast. Your plane shoots by itself; **Space** or **B** drops a bomb. Three hits makes you a ghost, and another plane can bring you back. Beat him and everyone still flying gets the gold star.
- **Interface — watching and pinning are one list:** ★ a thread in the Interface and it's pinned on the [s4s] catalog and in 4chan's Thread Watcher; pin or watch it there and the Interface follows.
- **Interface — green posts don't watch threads:** posting a green post in a board thread no longer adds it to your watchlist.
- **Interface — ▲ / ▼:** buttons in the bottom corner jump to the top or bottom of the thread list or a thread.
- **Interface — pictures and videos stay open:** a picture opened full size, a video or a YouTube embed no longer closes when the thread refreshes.
