## What's new in 0.5.0

- **The Hall.** Press 🕺 in the Chat window's title bar to walk into a little pixel room with everyone else listening. Pick your look (🎨 Look), walk around with the mouse, the keyboard or a controller, and wave, dance, jump, sit or cheer (keys 1–5). What you say comes out of your character's mouth, and it's the station chat, so the show sees it too.
- **Radioman runs the room** from his desk on the stage: he nods along to the music and talks when the show is on the mic.
